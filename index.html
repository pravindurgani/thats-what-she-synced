What it does.

Nobody sits in the dark.

When it’s quiet, a cast member keeps the notch company over a glow in its own colours. There are five. Shuffle deals a new one with every song.

Dancing. On the beat. In uniform.

While music plays, dancers hit a new pose on every beat, dressed in the album’s colours. Three numbers. Threat level: mid-tempo.

Claude left. Codex right.

When your agents work, each gets a wing, a comet ring and its own colours. You can see who’s busy without opening anything.

Your battery is a mug now.

Charging fills it with coffee. A call arrives as “Conference room. Now.” Charging, earphones, speakers, calls, messages and meetings each get a deadpan caption, in English only. The real information still comes first.

It knows when to stop.

Reduce Motion rests everything. Low Power Mode switches the glow off. Nothing animates while the notch is hidden or the screen sleeps.

Install in four steps.

There is no download. You build it from source, which sounds harder than it is.

You need an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 14.4 or later, plus Apple’s Command Line Tools:

xcode-select --install

Works with Apple Music, Spotify, and music in Safari, Chrome or Firefox.

  1. 1

    Build it:

    git clone https://github.com/pravindurgani/thats-what-she-synced.git
cd thats-what-she-synced
VORSSAINT_DEV_OPTIMIZE=1 ./build.sh --dev --install
  2. 2

    Let it hear the beat: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording → under System Audio Recording Only, turn on Vorssaint (Developer).

  3. 3

    Switch on, in the app’s Settings: Dynamic Island, Bars follow the music and Accessory alerts. For the cast and the agents, also install Companion and AI Agents on the Features page.

  4. 4

    Play a song and hover over the notch.

Two islands?
Also running the official Vorssaint? Quit it, so you don’t get two islands.
